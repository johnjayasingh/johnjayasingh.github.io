Tip Top Rider

A food-delivery bike game set on real Indian streets, from the village roads of Pollachi to Mumbai's monsoon traffic. I built it end to end, from the rendering engine to the store release.

  • 12 cities and 46 areas, each with its own landmarks and food
  • 42 bikes, from a ₹350 bicycle to a 300 km/h superbike
  • New in 1.4: one-tap Style Park, TURBO, the Rider Academy and four ways to ride
  • 300+ customers with moods, who remember how you treated them
  • Plays offline on Android and iPhone

How it's built

Rendering
2.5D Indian streets drawn with canvas and Three.js at 60 fps, with a battery mode for mid-range phones.
Native apps
One web codebase shipped to Android and iOS with Capacitor, plus my own native plugins in Swift and Java.
Payments
Subscriptions and coin packs through RevenueCat, and AdMob ads with consent handling.
Saves
Several rider profiles per phone, sealed against tampering and backed up to iCloud and Google Play Games.
