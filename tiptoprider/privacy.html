1.Who we are

Tip Top Rider: Delivery Game ("Tip Top Rider", the "game" or "app") is a mobile game published by John Jayasingh ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

2.Data kept on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • Game progress: wallet balance, XP and level, unlocked areas, bikes and gear you own, loans and EMIs, ratings and delivery history.
  • Settings such as sound and controls.
  • If you signed in: your account ID and display name (see section 3).

We do not receive this data. It is never sent to us, because the game has no server. The game works fully offline.

Cloud save. So your progress comes back after you reinstall the game or move to a new phone, the game also keeps a copy of the same game data in your own platform account: the iCloud key-value store on iPhone (when you are signed in to iCloud), or Google Play Games Saved Games on Android (when you are signed in to Play Games). Apple or Google stores that copy under your account and their privacy terms. Only this game on your devices can read it; we cannot. You can turn it off for this app in iCloud settings on iPhone, or by signing out of Play Games on Android.

Device backups. Your phone's operating system may include this app data in your own device backup (Android Auto Backup to your Google account, or iCloud device backup on iOS), depending on your phone's settings. Those backups are stored by Google or Apple under your account and their privacy terms. We cannot access them.

3.Optional sign-in with Google or Apple

Signing in is optional. Everything in the game works without it. If you choose Sign in with Google (Android and iOS) or Sign in with Apple (iOS):

  • Google or Apple shares your name, email address and a unique account ID with the app on your phone. With Apple you can choose to hide your email.
  • The app stores only your account ID and display name, on your device, to show who is signed in and to link your local profile to that identity. Your email address is not stored.
  • We do not send any of this to a server, and we do not use it for ads.
  • Google and Apple handle the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy.

We do not offer cloud save yet. If that changes, we will update this policy before it launches and tell you in the app.

The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between shifts, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares the following with Google:

The game does not give advertisers your game progress, name or account ID.

4a.In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

The game sells optional coin packs and Tip Top Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime). Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. To check that a purchase is genuine and to know whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random app user ID created by the app (not your name or email),
  • your purchase receipts and history for this app (product, price, currency, dates, renewal and refund status),
  • basic device and app information (platform, OS and app version, country derived from the store).

This is used only to deliver and restore your purchases, manage subscriptions and prevent fraud. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Google and Apple handle payments under their own policies. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings.

5.Consent and your ad choices

  • Consent message (Google UMP). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or in another region where consent or a privacy notice is required (including US states with privacy laws), the game shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. You can accept, decline, or choose by purpose. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will be non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Change your choice later. Open Settings → Privacy options in the game to see the consent form again and change your choices at any time.
  • iPhone and iPad. iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or to Google, and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Google's My Ad Center also lets you manage ad personalisation for your Google account.

6.What we do not collect

  • No GPS or precise location. The game never asks for location permission. The cities and streets in the game are drawn, not tracked.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, SMS or files.
  • No chat, multiplayer, friends lists or user-generated content.
  • No payment card or UPI details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store (see section 4a).
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. Only the diagnostics collected by the ads SDK described in section 4.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

7.Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete my data removes your local profile from the phone: progress, wallet, bikes, settings, and any linked account ID and display name. It also clears the cloud save copy (section 2). It cannot be undone. If you play on another phone with the same iCloud or Play Games account, that phone will back up its own progress again.
  • Sign out (in Settings) unlinks your Google or Apple account. The account ID and display name are removed from the device, and your guest progress stays.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the phone. Copies may remain in your own device backup until that backup is replaced or deleted in your Google or Apple account settings.
  • Remove the app's sign-in access at any time: Google, at myaccount.google.com/connections; Apple, in Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.
  • Ad data held by Google: reset your advertising ID (section 5) and use Google's controls. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete. If you have questions, contact us (section 12).

8.Children

Tip Top Rider is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is about a delivery job, traffic fines, loans and EMIs. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app itself stores data only on the device, and we have no means to identify a user's age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used sign-in or seen personalised ads in the game, contact us. We will help you use Delete my data and the ad controls above, and we will take any further steps the law requires.

9.Retention, security and transfers

  • Retention. Data saved by the game stays on your device until you use Delete my data, sign out, or uninstall. The cloud save copy stays in your iCloud or Play Games account until you use Delete my data, or remove it in your Apple or Google account settings. Google keeps ad data according to its own retention policies.
  • Security. Game data sits inside the app's private storage, protected by your phone's operating system. Communication by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • International transfers. Google may process ad data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under its own safeguards.

10.Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or US state privacy laws such as California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to withdraw consent, to object to or opt out of targeted advertising, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the game keeps your data on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (Delete my data, Sign out, Privacy options). For ad data, use Google's controls listed above. You can always contact us for help.

11.Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the game's data practices change, for example if we add new data uses, and we will change the effective date at the top. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the game before they take effect.
