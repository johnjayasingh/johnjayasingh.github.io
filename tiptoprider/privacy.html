1.Who we are

Tip Top Rider: Delivery Game ("Tip Top Rider", the "game" or "app") is a game published by John Jayasingh ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac versions of the game. On the Apple App Store the game may be listed under a different seller name; this policy still applies.

2.Data kept on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following only in the app's storage on your device:

  • Game progress: wallet balance, XP and level, unlocked areas, bikes and gear you own, loans and EMIs, ratings and delivery history.
  • Settings such as sound and controls.
  • If you signed in: your account ID and display name (see section 3).

We do not receive this data. It is never sent to us, because the game has no server. The game works fully offline.

Cloud save. So your progress comes back after you reinstall the game or move to a new device, the game also keeps a copy of the same game data in your own platform account: the iCloud key-value store on iPhone, iPad and Mac (when you are signed in to iCloud), or Google Play Games Saved Games on Android (when you are signed in to Play Games). Apple or Google stores that copy under your account and their privacy terms. Only this game on your devices can read it; we cannot. You can turn it off for this app in iCloud settings, or by signing out of Play Games on Android.

Device backups. Your phone's operating system may include this app data in your own device backup (Android Auto Backup to your Google account, or iCloud device backup on iOS), depending on your phone's settings. Those backups are stored by Google or Apple under your account and their privacy terms. We cannot access them.

3.Optional sign-in with Google or Apple

Signing in is optional. Everything in the game works without it. If you choose Sign in with Google (Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac) or Sign in with Apple (iPhone, iPad and Mac):

  • Google or Apple shares your name, email address and a unique account ID with the app on your device. With Apple you can choose to hide your email.
  • The app stores only your account ID and display name, on your device, to show who is signed in and to link your local profile to that identity. Your email address is not stored.
  • We do not send any of this to a server, and we do not use it for ads.
  • Google and Apple handle the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy.

Signing in is not needed for cloud save. Cloud save uses your own iCloud or Google Play Games account on the phone, as described in section 2, and never sends anything to us.

4.No ads and no purchases

The whole game is free. It shows no ads, has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or purchase-management services. Coins and bikes are earned only by playing and have no real-world value. On iPhone, iPad and Mac the game never asks to track you.

Earlier versions. Test versions up to 1.5 on Android showed ads from Google AdMob and offered optional purchases verified by RevenueCat. Those features have been removed. If you still have an older version installed, please update to the latest version from your store.

5.What we do not collect

  • No personal data is sent to us or to any third party by the game.
  • No GPS or precise location. The game never asks for location permission. The cities and streets in the game are drawn, not tracked.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, SMS or files. When you share a photo from the Photo shoot, your device's own share sheet sends it only where you choose.
  • The game reads the device's tilt (motion sensor) only so the Tilt controls can steer the bike. The readings are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • No chat, multiplayer, friends lists or user-generated content.
  • No advertising identifiers, ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • No payment details, because there is nothing to buy.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

6.Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete my data removes your local profile from the device: progress, wallet, bikes, settings, and any linked account ID and display name. It also clears the cloud save copy (section 2). It cannot be undone. If you play on another device with the same iCloud or Play Games account, that device will back up its own progress again.
  • Sign out (in Settings) unlinks your Google or Apple account. The account ID and display name are removed from the device, and your guest progress stays.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the device. Copies may remain in your own device backup until that backup is replaced or deleted in your Google or Apple account settings.
  • Remove the app's sign-in access at any time: Google, at myaccount.google.com/connections; Apple, in Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete. If you have questions, contact us (section 10).

7.Children

Tip Top Rider is a light-hearted game suitable for players of all ages. It has no ads, no purchases, no chat and no way to share personal information with other players, and we do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Sign-in with Google or Apple is optional and not needed to play; we recommend that children under 13 play as a guest or sign in only with a parent's help. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of the game, contact us and we will help you use Delete my data or Sign out.

8.Retention and security

  • Retention. Data saved by the game stays on your device until you use Delete my data, sign out, or uninstall. The cloud save copy stays in your iCloud or Play Games account until you use Delete my data, or remove it in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Security. Game data sits inside the app's private storage, protected by your device's operating system. Cloud save and sign-in are handled by Apple or Google over encrypted connections.

9.Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or US state privacy laws such as California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the game keeps your data only on your device and in your own cloud account, you can exercise these rights directly in the app (Delete my data, Sign out). You can always contact us for help.

10.Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the game's data practices change, and we will change the effective date at the top. If we ever add ads, purchases or any data collection, we will update this policy and let you know in the game before the change takes effect.
